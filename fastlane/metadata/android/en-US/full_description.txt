MFB Think Tank collects long-form analysis from think tanks and the policy press in one feed, and helps you get through it faster.

AI write-ups. Open any piece for a TL;DR, key points, why it matters and what to watch for. Pieces without a write-up can be summarized on demand with your own AI key.

A feed that learns. Give a thumbs up or down to any piece. After five ratings the feed re-ranks itself around the topics you care about, and the For You filter shows only what matches your taste.

Deep dives. Tap "Read more about it" on any story. MFB Think Tank gathers related coverage from across the web and has your model write a cited companion piece: background, competing interpretations, implications and what to watch. Results are saved to your account.

Search and filter. Search titles, topics and write-ups. Filter by think tanks or news, and narrow by topic: defense, energy, markets, technology, Europe, Asia Pacific and more.

Bring your own key. Connect an Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or other supported API key. Your key is encrypted at rest and used only for your own write-ups and deep dives.

MFB Think Tank links to every original source. Summaries are generated by AI and may contain errors; always read the original for anything important.

This is the free-software build. It contains no proprietary SDKs: no Firebase, no Google Play Services and no advertising or analytics libraries. It signs in against a self-hosted account service and talks to the MFB Think Tank API over HTTPS.
